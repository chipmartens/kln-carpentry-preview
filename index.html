Why KLN

Finish carpentry is the only trade whose whole job stays visible. Every reveal, every scribe, every mitre is there for as long as the house is. So I measure it, build it, install it, and come back to finish the list myself. Nothing gets handed off, which means nothing falls through the gap between the shop and the site.
